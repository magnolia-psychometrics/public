## This App (Synthometricon)

Synthometricon is a semantic search engine for psychometric items and scales, developed in the SYNTH research programme and published as a research preview. The Supplemental Research Preview Disclaimer applies in addition to this policy.

### Hosting on Hugging Face

The app runs on Hugging Face Spaces, operated by Hugging Face, Inc. (USA; main establishment in the EU: Hugging Face SAS, 9 rue des Colonnes, 75002 Paris, France). Every request to the app passes through Hugging Face's servers, which process your IP address, the requested address, date and time, and your browser and operating system (user agent). If you open the app on huggingface.co, that page is delivered by Hugging Face, which may set cookies there. Hugging Face processes this data under its own privacy policy (https://huggingface.co/privacy), and the processing may involve a transfer to the USA.

The app itself writes no access logs.

- **Purpose:** delivering the app.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in making the app publicly available.

### Searches and Shared Links

Searching requires your consent, which you give with the checkbox above the search form. For each search, we store on our own database server at Hetzner in Germany:

- the items or scale descriptions you searched for and the search mode;
- date and time of the search;
- a random identifier generated by your browser (see "Local Storage"), which groups the searches made in the same browser;
- the version of the terms you agreed to and when you agreed;
- how the search was started (search form or shared link) and which server instance handled it.

We do not store your IP address, name, or any other identifying details with a search. Each distinct search gets a short ID, which makes the search retrievable via a shared link (`?q=<ID>`) by anyone who has the link.

**Do not enter personal data** (e.g., names or information about identifiable people) into the search. The text you enter is stored as described above.

- **Purpose:** carrying out the search, providing shared links, and research on how psychometric content is searched, published only in aggregated form.
- **Legal basis:** your consent, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time, with effect for the future, by unticking the consent checkbox in the app (after you have agreed, open it via "Review"). Searches made before the withdrawal remain stored; since they are not linked to your name, we can only delete them if you send us the shared link or ID of the search.
- **Retention:** as long as needed for the research purposes described here and for shared links to keep working.

### Local Storage

The app does not set cookies. It keeps the following entries in your browser's local storage:

- `synthometricon_consent`: the version of the terms you agreed to and when you agreed, so you are not asked again until the terms change;
- `synthometricon_queries`: your last search, so it is still there when you return; it stays on your device;
- `synthometricon_session_id`: the random identifier described under "Searches and Shared Links".

- **Legal basis:** storing the session identifier is based on your consent (Section 25 (1) German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG); storing your consent and your last search is strictly necessary to provide the app as you use it (Section 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG).
- You can delete these entries at any time in your browser settings.

### Audience Measurement with Umami

To understand how the app is used, we use the open-source software Umami. It runs on our own servers at Hetzner in Germany, and no data is passed to third parties. Umami does not set cookies and stores nothing on your device.

For each page view, we record: the page (without the part of the address after `?`, so shared-link IDs are not recorded) and page title, referrer URL, browser, operating system, device type, screen resolution, language setting, and the country, region, and city derived from the IP address. The IP address itself is not stored. To group page views into visits, Umami computes an identifier from the IP address, browser details, and a regularly changing random value. The IP address cannot be recovered from this identifier, and visits cannot be linked to the same person over longer periods. Umami data is not linked to your searches.

For a random sample of visits, Umami also records click and scroll positions (heatmaps): where on the page a click lands, how far a page is scrolled, and the size of the page and browser window. What you type (including your search queries), the text of the page, and mouse movements are not recorded, and we do not record visits as replays. We only view this data aggregated across many visits, to see which parts of the app are used.

If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, no audience measurement takes place.

- **Purpose:** statistical analysis of usage to improve the app.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in privacy-friendly audience measurement.
- **Retention:** The statistics data is deleted after 24 months.

### Other Content

Apart from the Hugging Face hosting described above, all fonts, scripts, and other resources are delivered with the app; no content is loaded from other third parties. Links to external sites (e.g., GitHub, OSF, DOI links to publications) are plain links: data is only transmitted to the respective provider if you follow them.
