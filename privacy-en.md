## Our Website (magnolia-psychometrics.com)

### Hosting

Our website is hosted by a hosting provider on servers in Germany. The provider processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.

### Server Log Files

Every time you access our website, your browser automatically transmits information that is processed in server log files: IP address, date and time of the request, requested page or file, HTTP status code, amount of data transferred, referrer URL, and browser and operating system (user agent).

- **Purpose:** delivering the website, ensuring its stability and security, and investigating errors and attacks.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in a secure and functioning website.
- **Retention:** Log files are deleted automatically after 7 days at the latest. Data whose further retention is required as evidence of a security incident is exempt from deletion until the incident has been resolved.

### Language Preference (Cookie and Local Storage)

To display the website in your language, we store your language preference (the value "en" or "de") in a cookie named `lang` and in your browser's local storage. The value is derived from your browser's language setting or your choice in the language menu, is kept for one year, and is not used for any other purpose.

- **Legal basis:** Storing this information on your device is strictly necessary to provide the language selection you requested (Section 25 (2) No. 2 German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). The associated processing is based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.
- You can delete the cookie and the local storage entry at any time in your browser settings.

On our website, we do not set any other cookies. If you play a YouTube video, YouTube may store cookies or similar information on your device (see "YouTube Videos").

### Contact Form

When you write to us via the contact form, we send your details (name, email address, optionally your organization, and your message) together with the page language by email to our mailbox. The details are not stored on the web server itself. Our mailbox is operated by Google Cloud EMEA Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (Google Workspace), under a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR. This may involve a transfer to the USA, which is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

To protect against automated messages, we limit the number of messages per IP address. For this purpose, we store a hash of your IP address (a string derived from it that does not directly reveal the address) on our server for at most one hour.

- **Legal basis and retention of your message:** see "Contacting Us".
- **Legal basis of the per-IP limit:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in a contact form protected against abuse.

### Audience Measurement with Umami

To understand which content is read, we use the open-source software Umami. It runs on our own servers at our hosting provider in Germany (see "Hosting"), and no data is passed to third parties. Umami does not set cookies and stores nothing on your device.

For each page view, we record: the page and page title, referrer URL, browser, operating system, device type, screen resolution, language setting, and the country, region, and city derived from the IP address. We also count individual actions in blog articles, such as sharing an article, copying a citation, or reading to the end. The IP address itself is not stored. To group page views into visits, Umami computes an identifier from the IP address, browser details, and a regularly changing random value. The IP address cannot be recovered from this identifier, and visits cannot be linked to the same person over longer periods.

For a random sample of visits, Umami also records click and scroll positions (heatmaps): where on the page a click lands, how far a page is scrolled, and the size of the page and browser window. What you type, the text of the page, and mouse movements are not recorded, and we do not record visits as replays. We only view this data aggregated across many visits, to see which parts of a page are used.

If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, no audience measurement takes place.

- **Purpose:** statistical analysis of usage to improve our content and website.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in privacy-friendly audience measurement.
- **Retention:** The statistics data is deleted after 24 months.

### Third-Party Content

### YouTube Videos

Some articles contain videos hosted on YouTube, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Opening such an article does not establish any connection to YouTube: we only show a preview image stored on our own server. The video is loaded from YouTube only when you click play. YouTube is embedded in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).

When you click play, your browser connects to servers of YouTube and Google. At a minimum, your IP address, the address of the page, and technical information about your browser are transmitted. YouTube may also store cookies or similar information on your device, use data for its own purposes (e.g., analysis or advertising), and link it to your Google account if you are logged in. We have no influence on this processing and do not know its full scope. For details, see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

- **Legal basis:** your consent, which you give by clicking play (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR; Section 25 (1) TDDDG). Your consent applies to the video you start and is not stored; every further video requires a new click.
- **Withdrawal:** You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future by stopping playback and leaving or reloading the page. From then on, our page transmits no further data to YouTube. Data already transmitted is subject to Google's privacy policy.
- **Transfer to third countries:** Data may be transferred to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

### Interactive Examples

Some articles contain interactive examples whose calculations run entirely in your browser. The required program files are loaded from our server. The values you enter are processed locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

### Searches and Shared Links

Searching requires your consent, which you give with the checkbox above the search form. For each search, we store on our own database server in Germany:

- the items or scale descriptions you searched for and the search mode;
- date and time of the search;
- a random identifier generated by your browser (see "Local Storage"), which groups the searches made in the same browser;
- the version of the terms you agreed to and when you agreed;
- how the search was started (search form or shared link) and which server instance handled it.

We do not store your IP address, name, or any other identifying details with a search. Each distinct search gets a short ID, which makes the search retrievable via a shared link (`?q=<ID>`) by anyone who has the link.

**Do not enter personal data** (e.g., names or information about identifiable people) into the search. The text you enter is stored as described above.

- **Purpose:** carrying out the search, providing shared links, and research on how psychometric content is searched, published only in aggregated form.
- **Legal basis:** your consent, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time, with effect for the future, by unticking the consent checkbox in the app (after you have agreed, open it via "Review"). Searches made before the withdrawal remain stored; since they are not linked to your name, we can only delete them if you send us the shared link or ID of the search.
- **Retention:** as long as needed for the research purposes described here and for shared links to keep working.

### Local Storage

The app does not set cookies. It keeps the following entries in your browser's local storage:

- `synthometricon_consent`: the version of the terms you agreed to and when you agreed, so you are not asked again until the terms change;
- `synthometricon_queries`: your last search, so it is still there when you return; it stays on your device;
- `synthometricon_session_id`: the random identifier described under "Searches and Shared Links".

- **Legal basis:** storing the session identifier is based on your consent (Section 25 (1) German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG); storing your consent and your last search is strictly necessary to provide the app as you use it (Section 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG).
- You can delete these entries at any time in your browser settings.
