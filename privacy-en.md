## Contacting Us

If you contact us by email, phone, or otherwise, we process the data you provide (e.g., name, email address, organization, the content of your message) to handle your request.

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract or to pre-contractual measures; otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in answering inquiries addressed to us.
- **Retention:** We delete the data once your request has been dealt with conclusively, unless statutory retention obligations apply (see below).

## Clients and Business Partners

When we prepare, conclude, and perform contracts, we process the contact and contract data of our clients, business partners, and their contact persons (e.g., name, business contact details, role, correspondence, order and billing data).

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (contract), Art. 6 (1) (c) GDPR (statutory obligations, e.g., under tax and commercial law), and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR for communication with contact persons of companies and institutions.
- **Retention:** For the duration of the business relationship, and thereafter as long as statutory retention obligations under commercial and tax law require (generally up to ten years).

Where we process personal data on behalf of our clients (e.g., data of participants in assessments or studies), our client is the controller for this processing, and we act as a processor under a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR. Information about that processing is provided by our client.

## Recipients

We only disclose personal data where this is necessary for the purposes described here. Recipients are in particular:

- service providers who process data on our behalf (e.g., hosting, IT and email services), bound by data processing agreements pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR;
- tax advisors, auditors, and authorities, where we are legally required to do so or this is necessary to assert or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal data.

## Transfers to Third Countries

We do not transfer personal data to countries outside the European Economic Area unless this is stated for a specific processing activity in this policy. Where such a transfer takes place, it is based on an adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR) or on standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR).

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR);
- **rectification** of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR);
- **erasure** of your data (Art. 17 GDPR);
- **restriction** of processing (Art. 18 GDPR);
- **data portability** (Art. 20 GDPR);
- **withdraw** any consent you have given, with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3) GDPR).

**Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR):** Where we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interests (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR), you have the right to object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights, and freedoms, or the processing serves to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. You may object to the use of your data for direct marketing at any time without giving reasons.

To exercise your rights, an informal message to hello@magnolia-pm.com is sufficient.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is: Sächsische Datenschutz- und Transparenzbeauftragte, Devrientstraße 5, 01067 Dresden, Germany.

## Data Security

We protect personal data with appropriate technical and organizational measures. Our websites are delivered exclusively over encrypted connections (TLS).

## Automated Decision-Making

We do not use automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

## Obligation to Provide Data

You are neither legally nor contractually obliged to provide personal data. Without the data, however, we may not be able to answer your request or to conclude or perform a contract.

## Changes to This Policy

We update this privacy policy when our processing or the legal situation changes. The current version is always available on the respective website.
