### Server Log Files

Every time you access this website, your browser automatically transmits information that is processed in server log files: IP address, date and time of the request, requested page or file, HTTP status code, amount of data transferred, referrer URL, and browser and operating system (user agent).

- **Purpose:** delivering the website, ensuring its stability and security, and investigating errors and attacks.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in a secure and functioning website.
- **Retention:** Log files are deleted automatically after 7 days at the latest. Data whose further retention is required as evidence of a security incident is exempt from deletion until the incident has been resolved.

### Language Preference (Cookie and Local Storage)

To display the website in your language, we store your language preference (the value "en" or "de") in a cookie named `lang` and in your browser's local storage. The value is derived from your browser's language setting or your choice in the language menu, is kept for one year, and is not used for any other purpose.

- **Legal basis:** Storing this information on your device is strictly necessary to provide the language selection you requested (Section 25 (2) No. 2 German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). The associated processing is based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.
- You can delete the cookie and the local storage entry at any time in your browser settings.

We do not use any other cookies.

### Audience Measurement with Umami

To understand which content is read, we use the open-source software Umami. It runs on our own servers at Hetzner (see "Hosting"), and no data is passed to third parties. Umami does not set cookies and stores nothing on your device.

For each page view, we record: the page and page title, referrer URL, browser, operating system, device type, screen resolution, language setting, and the country, region, and city derived from the IP address. We also count individual actions in blog articles, such as sharing an article, copying a citation, or reading to the end. The IP address itself is not stored. To group page views into visits, Umami computes an identifier from the IP address, browser details, and a regularly changing random value. The IP address cannot be recovered from this identifier, and visits cannot be linked to the same person over longer periods.

For a random sample of visits, Umami also records click and scroll positions (heatmaps): where on the page a click lands, how far a page is scrolled, and the size of the page and browser window. What you type, the text of the page, and mouse movements are not recorded, and we do not record visits as replays. We only view this data aggregated across many visits, to see which parts of a page are used.

If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, no audience measurement takes place.

- **Purpose:** statistical analysis of usage to improve our content and website.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in privacy-friendly audience measurement.
- **Retention:** The statistics data is deleted after 24 months.
