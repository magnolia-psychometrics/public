## This Website (magnolia-psychometrics.com)

### Hosting

This website is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, on servers located in Germany. Hetzner processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.

### Server Log Files

Every time you access this website, your browser automatically transmits information that is processed in server log files: IP address, date and time of the request, requested page or file, HTTP status code, amount of data transferred, referrer URL, and browser and operating system (user agent).

- **Purpose:** delivering the website, ensuring its stability and security, and investigating errors and attacks.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in a secure and functioning website.
- **Retention:** Log files are deleted automatically after 7 days at the latest. Data whose further retention is required as evidence of a security incident is exempt from deletion until the incident has been resolved.

### Language Preference (Cookie and Local Storage)

To display the website in your language, we store your language preference (the value "en" or "de") in a cookie named `lang` and in your browser's local storage. The value is derived from your browser's language setting or your choice in the language menu, is kept for one year, and is not used for any other purpose.

- **Legal basis:** Storing this information on your device is strictly necessary to provide the language selection you requested (Section 25 (2) No. 2 German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). The associated processing is based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.
- You can delete the cookie and the local storage entry at any time in your browser settings.

We do not use any other cookies.

### No Tracking, No Third-Party Content

This website does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services. All fonts, scripts, and other resources are delivered from our own server; no content is loaded from third parties when you visit the website. Links to external platforms (e.g., LinkedIn, GitHub, Hugging Face) are plain links: data is only transmitted to the respective provider if you follow them.

### Interactive Examples

Some articles contain interactive examples whose calculations run entirely in your browser. The required program files are loaded from our server. The values you enter are processed locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.
